Kipas 1.4.1 picks up where you left off after locking.

Installed copies update themselves (Settings → Updates → Check for updates).

### Downloads

- **Windows:** `Kipas-Setup-1.4.1.exe`
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Kipas-1.4.1-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Linux:** `Kipas-1.4.1-x86_64.AppImage`

### Changed

- **Unlocking brings you back to where you were** instead of All items: the same group, type, Favorites, Trash or
  Health page, your search and sort, and the item you had selected, ready for the keyboard. Dialogs and revealed
  passwords stay closed. Opening a different vault, or restarting Kipas, still starts on All items.

The builds are not code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. On
macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
