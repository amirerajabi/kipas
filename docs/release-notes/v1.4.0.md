Kipas 1.4 brings groups inside groups, and auto-type now signs you in with Enter.

Installed copies update themselves (Settings → Updates → Check for updates).

### Downloads

- **Windows:** `Kipas-Setup-1.4.0.exe`
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Kipas-1.4.0-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Linux:** `Kipas-1.4.0-x86_64.AppImage`

### New

- **Groups inside groups,** as deep as you like. Right-click a group and choose **New subgroup…**, or drag a group
  onto another to nest it (drop it on the **Groups** heading to bring it back to the top). The side panel shows the
  tree and remembers which groups you closed.
  - Selecting a group shows its items and those of its subgroups; the count beside it includes them too.
  - Paths read like **Work / Clients / Acme** in the details, and search finds items by any part of the path.
  - Deleting a group moves its items and subgroups up one level; nothing is lost.
- **KeePass imports keep the whole group tree,** empty groups included. CSV imports turn paths such as
  `Root/Internet/Mail` and nested Bitwarden folders into nested groups, and CSV export writes the paths.

### Changed

- **Auto-type presses Enter at the end** by default: user name, Tab, password, Enter. If you never changed the
  sequence, you get the new one automatically. Items with their own sequence keep it.

Groups imported by earlier versions with names like "Internet / Social" stay as they are; drag them into place, or
import the KeePass file again.

The builds are not code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen may warn on first run: choose **More info → Run anyway**. On
macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
